Questions families ask

Will my parent find it difficult?

We designed their side around one button. The text is large, there are no tabs or swipes to learn, and the app never shows them ads or asks them to buy anything. Setting up their phone takes a 6-digit code from yours, with no email address or password for them to remember.

What if they forget to tap?

They get a gentle reminder when the window opens and another before it closes. Only after that does the family hear anything. You choose how long to wait, and you can pause everything for a holiday or a hospital stay.

Does it track where they are?

No. Morning Wave never asks for location permission on any phone. There is no map, no history of places, and no way for anyone to see where your parent has been. That is deliberate.

What if their phone is off or has no signal?

The tap is saved on their phone and sent as soon as they are back online, keeping the time it actually happened. The alerts run on our servers, so they still arrive even when nobody has the app open. If their phone stops reporting in, the family sees a warning on the dashboard.

Can we cover both our parents?

Yes. Each parent gets their own circle, with their own schedule and their own phone. One Morning Wave Family subscription covers up to five circles, so one of you paying covers both parents and all the siblings.

Is it an emergency service?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. Morning Wave alerts the people you choose. It does not call an ambulance, does not monitor health, and cannot promise a notification will arrive in time. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Who can see my parent's information?

Only the people in their circle: the family members and backup contacts you invite. We don't sell personal information, and you can delete your account and data at any time, in the app or on our website. Our privacy policy sets out the details.
